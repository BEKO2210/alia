`cache.py` contains an LRU cache. Add a time-to-live: `LRUCache(capacity, ttl=None, clock=time.monotonic)`. With a ttl (seconds), an entry expires `ttl` seconds after it was last *written*; reading does not extend it. Expired entries behave as missing for `get` (returns the default), `__contains__` and `__len__`, and must not count against capacity when a new key is inserted. Keep the existing LRU behaviour and tests.
